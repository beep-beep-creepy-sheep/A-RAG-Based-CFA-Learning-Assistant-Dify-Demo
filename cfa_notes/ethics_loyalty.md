# Ethics: Loyalty

## Definition

Loyalty means investment professionals must place client interests before their own interests or the interests of their employer when managing client assets or making recommendations.

## Formula

There is no mathematical formula. The decision logic is:

```text
Client interests first + Full conflict disclosure + Fair dealing
```

## Intuition

Clients rely on investment professionals to act with care, honesty, and good faith. Loyalty requires avoiding self-dealing, managing conflicts, and ensuring clients are treated fairly.

## Common Trap

Do not assume disclosure alone fixes every conflict. Some conflicts must be avoided or managed, not merely disclosed.

Another trap is thinking loyalty to the employer can override client duties. Employer duties matter, but they do not justify harming clients or market integrity.

## Mini Example

An analyst owns shares in a company they are about to recommend. They should disclose the conflict according to firm policy and applicable standards. They should not let personal holdings influence the recommendation.
